SIP Calculator Guide: How Monthly SIP Investments Grow

Learn how SIP investment returns are estimated, how monthly contributions and time affect wealth creation, and why market-linked returns are not guaranteed.

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly, commonly into mutual fund schemes. SIPs can help investors build a disciplined investment habit.

How does a SIP calculator work?

A SIP calculator estimates the future value of regular investments using an assumed rate of return. It is a mathematical projection, not a promise of what a mutual fund will actually return.

What has the biggest impact?

  • Monthly investment: increasing the contribution can increase the projected corpus.
  • Time: a longer investment period gives compounding more time to work.
  • Return assumption: a higher assumed return increases the mathematical estimate, but actual market returns vary.
  • Consistency: regular investing can make it easier to follow a long-term plan.

Example

If you invest ₹5,000 every month for several years, the final value depends heavily on the assumed return and investment period. Use the SIP calculator on the Smart Money homepage to test different scenarios rather than relying on a single projection.

Important things to remember

  • Mutual fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.
  • Past performance does not guarantee future performance.
  • Inflation reduces the future purchasing power of money.
  • Consider your goal, time horizon and risk tolerance before investing.
