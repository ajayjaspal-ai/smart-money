How Much Emergency Fund Should You Keep?

An emergency fund helps cover unexpected expenses or income disruption. The right target depends on essential monthly spending, income stability and household responsibilities.

A simple starting framework

Calculate essential monthly expenses such as housing, food, utilities, basic transport, insurance and minimum debt obligations. Then multiply that amount by your chosen number of months.

Emergency fund target = essential monthly expenses × target months.

How many months?

There is no universal number. A household with stable income and low fixed obligations may need a different buffer from one with variable income, dependants or high monthly debt payments.

Where should it be kept?

Prioritise accessibility and capital preservation rather than chasing high returns. Choose accessible, suitable options based on your circumstances.
