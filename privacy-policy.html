Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Smart Money ("we", "our", or "the website") provides free financial calculators and educational information. We aim to collect as little personal information as reasonably possible.

The calculator tools on Smart Money are designed to process the values you enter in your browser to produce estimates. The website does not require an account to use the calculators, and the calculator pages are not designed to send your entered financial figures to us for storage.

Google may process technical data and use cookies or similar technologies under its own terms and privacy policy. Analytics data is used to understand site performance and improve the website; Smart Money does not use it to identify you personally.

Smart Money also includes Google AdSense advertising code. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your device, measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted by your settings and applicable rules, personalise advertising based on visits to this or other websites. Ad content and data processing are handled by Google and relevant third parties, not directly by Smart Money.

You can review or change Google's advertising personalisation choices through Google My Ad Center and review Google's data practices in its Privacy Policy. Browser settings may also let you restrict or delete cookies; disabling them may affect some site features or measurement.

Information entered into calculators

Calculator values are intended to be processed in your browser to produce estimates. Smart Money does not require an account, and the calculator tools are not designed to send the financial figures you enter to us for storage. However, normal website requests, analytics events and third-party advertising may process technical usage information as described above. Please avoid entering account numbers, passwords, OTPs or other sensitive personal information.

Smart Money may link to external websites, financial institutions, government resources or other third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

Smart Money is a general financial education website and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, analytics, advertising or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Important: Smart Money provides educational estimates and does not provide personalized financial, tax or investment advice.
