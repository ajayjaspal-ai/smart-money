How to use it

Build your safety buffer

1. Enter essential monthly expenses—not every optional purchase.

2. Select a target period. A 3-month target can be a starting milestone; 6–12 months may suit some households better.

3. Add only money already reserved for emergencies, then enter a realistic monthly contribution.

Note: This calculator provides an estimate for education and planning. It does not connect to your bank or provide personalized financial advice. Actual needs vary by household and circumstances.
