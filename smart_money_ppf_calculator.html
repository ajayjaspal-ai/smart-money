PPF GUIDE

How does the PPF calculation work?

This calculator estimates the future value of yearly PPF contributions using an assumed annual interest rate and annual compounding. PPF interest is governed by government rules, so the actual rate and account calculation should always be checked against the latest official notification.

Why the rate is editable

PPF interest rates can be revised by the Government of India. Keeping the rate editable means you can update the calculator when an official rate changes.

Planning tips

  • Use an amount you can contribute consistently.
  • Check the current government rules for contribution limits, withdrawals and extensions.
  • Compare the estimated maturity with your long-term goals.

This is a mathematical estimate, not an official PPF maturity quote. Government rules and rates prevail.
