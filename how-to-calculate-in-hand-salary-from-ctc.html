How to Calculate In-Hand Salary from CTC in India

Cost to Company (CTC) is the employer's total annual cost for an employee. It is not always the amount credited to your bank account. In-hand salary is the amount paid after applicable employee deductions and payroll adjustments.

CTC vs gross salary vs take-home pay

  • CTC: May include fixed pay, employer contributions, insurance, bonus and variable benefits.
  • Gross salary: Salary before employee-side deductions and tax withholding, as defined by the pay structure.
  • In-hand salary: The net amount paid after applicable deductions such as employee PF, professional tax where applicable, TDS and other payroll items.

How to estimate monthly take-home pay

  1. Check the detailed annual salary breakup in your offer letter.
  2. Separate fixed monthly salary from annual bonus, variable pay and non-cash benefits.
  3. Identify employee PF and other payroll deductions shown in the salary structure.
  4. Estimate tax withholding based on the applicable financial year, tax regime and declarations.
  5. Convert annual amounts to monthly amounts only where they are paid evenly through the year.

There is no single take-home figure for every ₹6 lakh CTC offer: employers may structure compensation differently, and deductions vary.

A practical way to read a salary offer

When comparing two job offers, do not compare only the headline CTC. Ask for the fixed-pay breakup, employer PF contribution, gratuity (if included), insurance value, annual bonus and variable-pay conditions. These items can be treated differently in the monthly payslip.

Illustrative monthly calculation

Suppose a payslip shows ₹42,000 gross monthly pay. If employee PF is ₹1,800, professional tax is ₹200 and TDS is ₹1,000, the estimated take-home amount would be ₹39,000 (₹42,000 − ₹1,800 − ₹200 − ₹1,000). This is only a made-up example; your deductions and tax may be different.

Checklist before estimating in-hand salary

  • Use the salary breakup from your offer letter, not only the CTC headline.
  • Confirm whether bonus and variable pay are guaranteed or performance-linked.
  • Check whether the PF amount shown is employee contribution, employer contribution, or both.
  • Use the actual monthly TDS estimate supplied by payroll or a qualified tax professional.
  • Compare annual take-home pay as well as monthly pay when some components are paid yearly.

Frequently asked questions

Is monthly CTC the same as monthly in-hand salary?

No. CTC can include employer-paid benefits and annual or variable components that are not credited to your bank every month.

Can I calculate exact in-hand salary from CTC alone?

Usually not. You need the salary breakup, applicable deductions, tax regime and payroll details to make a reliable estimate.

Why can two people with the same CTC receive different take-home pay?

Their salary structure, tax declarations, PF treatment, benefits and other payroll deductions may differ.
