How does a Home Loan EMI work?

Home loan EMI depends mainly on the amount borrowed, annual interest rate and repayment tenure. A longer tenure can reduce the monthly EMI but may increase total interest over the full loan period.

What should you compare?

  • Interest rate and whether it is fixed or floating.
  • Processing charges, insurance and other applicable fees.
  • Prepayment or foreclosure conditions.
  • Your total repayment, not only the monthly EMI.

Example
