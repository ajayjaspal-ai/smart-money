Debt Payoff Guide: Snowball vs Avalanche Method

Learn how to organise multiple debts, compare the debt snowball and debt avalanche methods, and build a practical debt repayment plan.

Start with a complete debt list

Write down every debt, outstanding balance, interest rate and required monthly payment. Seeing the full picture makes it easier to choose a repayment strategy.

Debt avalanche

The avalanche method generally directs extra money toward the debt with the highest interest rate first, while maintaining required payments on other debts. This can reduce interest mathematically when the assumptions are accurate.

Debt snowball

The snowball method directs extra money toward the smallest balance first. Paying off a small debt quickly can create a sense of progress and may make the plan easier to follow.

Which method is better?

There is no single method that is best for every person. Avalanche can be attractive when reducing interest is the priority. Snowball can be useful when quick psychological wins help you stay consistent.

Before taking another loan

  • Check whether the new EMI fits comfortably into your monthly cash flow.
  • Compare the total cost, not only the advertised rate.
  • Understand processing charges, penalties and prepayment terms.
  • Avoid using new high-cost debt simply to postpone an existing problem.
