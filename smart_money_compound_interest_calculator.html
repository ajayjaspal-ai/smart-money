5. How Compound Interest Works

Compound growth allows accumulated interest to become part of the balance used for future calculations. Regular contributions can also increase the projected value over time.

Important: This is a mathematical projection, not a guaranteed return. Actual rates, fees, taxes and investment outcomes depend on the product and applicable terms. Market-linked returns are not guaranteed.

6. Simple vs Compound Interest

Simple interest generally uses the original principal for interest calculations. Compound interest allows accumulated interest to contribute to the balance used for future calculations.
