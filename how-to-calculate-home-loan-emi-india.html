How to Calculate Home Loan EMI in India

Home loan EMI is one of the most important numbers to understand before borrowing. This guide explains the EMI formula, loan tenure, interest rate and total repayment.

What is home loan EMI?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the scheduled monthly payment used to repay a loan over a selected tenure. A typical EMI contains both interest and principal.

Home loan EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n−1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly instalments.

Does a longer tenure reduce EMI?

Usually yes, because repayment is spread over more months. However, a longer tenure can increase total interest paid over the life of the loan. Compare both EMI and total interest.

Other costs to consider

  • Down payment
  • Processing and lender charges
  • Stamp duty and registration where applicable
  • Insurance or product-linked costs
  • Prepayment conditions
